## Green kale smoothie

![Green kale smoothie](/uploads/2022/09/green-kale-smoothie.jpg)

Blend 1 cup of almond milk, 1 big handful of kale (stems removed), 1 frozen banana, ½ cup of frozen pineapple and 1 tablespoon of chia seeds until smooth.

## Green spinach smoothie

![Green spinach smoothie](/uploads/2022/09/green-spinach-smoothie.jpg)

Blend 1 cup of coconut water, 2 handfuls of spinach, 1 cup of frozen mango, ½ avocado and a squeeze of lime.

## Homemade almond milk

![Fresh homemade almond milk](/uploads/2022/09/fresh-vegan-dairy-free-almond-milk-with-craft-P8L93PE.jpg)

Soak 1 cup of almonds overnight, then drain. Blend them with 4 cups of fresh water, 1 to 2 dates and a pinch of salt. Strain through a nut milk bag, and keep it in the fridge for up to 3 days.

## Raw vegan coconut bites

![Raw vegan coconut bites](/uploads/2022/09/raw-vegan-coconut-bites.jpg)

Pulse 1 cup of shredded coconut, ½ cup of almonds, 6 soft dates and a splash of vanilla in a food processor. Roll the mixture into balls and chill.

## Raw raspberry cake

![Raspberry cake](/uploads/2022/09/raspberry-cake.jpg)

For the crust, press dates and nuts into a pan. For the filling, blend soaked cashews, coconut oil, maple syrup, lemon and raspberries. Pour the filling over the crust and freeze it until it's set.

## Our philosophy

![Fresh vegetables](/uploads/2022/09/veggies.png)

We believe in plants first, simple ingredients, and food that makes you feel good. Our recipes lean on vegetables, keep added sugar low, and leave room to experiment. Swap ingredients to suit your taste and what's in season.
