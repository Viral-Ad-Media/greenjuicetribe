*Last updated: November 13, 2026*

This policy explains what information Green Juice Tribe ("we") collects when you visit greenjuicetribe.com, and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- **Basic server logs.** Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is used to keep the site secure and working.
- **Cookies.** The public site does not use advertising cookies. The site's administrators use a sign-in cookie to access the admin area.
- **Affiliate links.** When you click a link to a retailer such as Amazon, that retailer may set its own cookies to track the referral. Their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

## How we use information

We use technical information only to operate, secure and improve the site. We don't sell your personal information.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The public site works without them.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the site owner.
