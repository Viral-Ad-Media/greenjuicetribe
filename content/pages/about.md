Green Juice Tribe started with a simple goal: make it easier for people to drink more fruits and vegetables. That means choosing the right juicer or blender, and actually enjoying what comes out of it.

## What we do

- **Buying guides** for juicers and blenders, organized by type and budget, so you can find a machine that fits your kitchen and routine.
- **Recipes** for green juices, smoothies and plant-based treats that taste good, not just "healthy".
- **How-tos** for the practical parts, like cleaning your juicer and storing smoothies overnight.

## How we choose products

We look at what people care about day to day: how well a machine handles leafy greens, how long cleanup takes, how loud it is, how much counter space it needs, and how long the warranty runs. We revisit our guides regularly and update them when better options appear.

## A note on health

We're juice and smoothie enthusiasts, not doctors. Our content is general information and isn't medical advice. If you have a health condition, talk to a qualified professional before making big changes to your diet.
