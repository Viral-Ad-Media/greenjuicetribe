Juice cleanses promise fast results, and the scale often does drop over a few days. It helps to understand why, so you can decide whether a cleanse fits your goals and set it up in a way that's sustainable.

![Best juice cleanse for weight loss](/uploads/2026/07/best-juice-cleanse-for-weight-loss-2026-thumbnail.jpg)

> **Important:** This article is general information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before starting a cleanse, especially if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, kidney disease or a history of disordered eating, or take any medication.

## What happens during a juice cleanse

- **Calories drop sharply.** Most juice-only days come in well below normal intake, which causes short-term weight loss.
- **Water weight changes.** Lower carbohydrate stores and less salt mean you lose water. That comes back when you eat normally again.
- **Protein and fiber are low.** Juice removes most fiber and contains little protein, so you may feel hungry and tired.

**Bottom line:** A short cleanse can be a reset, but lasting weight loss comes from habits you can keep, not from three days of juice.

## A better approach: the "juice plus" plan

Instead of juice only, swap one or two meals or snacks for fresh, vegetable-heavy juice, and keep the rest of your day built around whole foods.

**Sample day**

| Time | What to have |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | Green juice + eggs, tofu scramble or Greek yogurt |
| Lunch | Big salad with beans, chicken or fish, and olive oil |
| Snack | Green juice or a fiber-rich smoothie |
| Dinner | Vegetables, lean protein and whole grains |

This keeps protein and fiber up, controls hunger, and still adds several servings of vegetables a day.

## Juice recipes for a weight-conscious plan

Keep juices **mostly vegetables** with little fruit.

- **Lean Green:** cucumber, celery, spinach, lemon, ginger.
- **Zesty Greens:** kale, cucumber, ½ green apple, lime, mint.
- **Red Root:** small beet, carrots, celery, lemon.

## Buying a pre-made cleanse? What to look for

- **Vegetable-first ingredients** and low sugar per bottle.
- **Cold-pressed and dated,** with a short best-by date.
- **Transparent nutrition labels** that show calories, sugar and protein.
- **Plans that include food or protein,** not just six bottles of fruit juice.
