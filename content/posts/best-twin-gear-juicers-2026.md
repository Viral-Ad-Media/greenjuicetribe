Twin gear juicers, also called triturating juicers, use two interlocking gears that turn slowly toward each other. Produce is crushed and pressed between them, which gives exceptional yields from leafy greens, wheatgrass and herbs. These are the machines dedicated green juice drinkers often end up with.

They're heavier, more expensive and slower to use than other juicers, so they make the most sense if you juice greens every day.

## Is a twin gear juicer right for you?

**Yes, if** you juice greens or wheatgrass daily, care about yield, and don't mind spending extra time on prep and cleaning.

**No, if** you mostly juice fruit and carrots, or you want speed. A [centrifugal juicer](/best-centrifugal-juicers-2023/) or a vertical [masticating juicer](/best-masticating-juicers-2026/) will be easier to live with.
