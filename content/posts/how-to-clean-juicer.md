The number one reason people stop juicing is cleanup. The good news is that a juicer cleaned right away takes about five minutes. One left on the counter for a few hours can take half an hour of scrubbing dried pulp out of a mesh screen.

![Clean your juicer properly](/uploads/2026/07/clean-your-juicer-properly-complete-maintenance-thumbnail-1.jpg)

## The 5-minute daily clean

1. **Unplug the juicer** and take it apart as soon as you've poured your juice.
2. **Empty the pulp** into your compost bin, or save it for muffins, crackers or veggie broth.
3. **Rinse every part under warm running water**, starting with the screen or strainer while the pulp is still wet.
4. **Scrub the screen** with the brush that came with your juicer, or with a soft toothbrush. Brush from the inside out, following the direction of the holes.
5. **Wash the remaining parts** with warm water and a drop of mild dish soap.
6. **Air-dry** everything on a rack before you put it back together.

> Tip: Some people run a little water through the juicer right after juicing. It loosens pulp before you take the machine apart.

## The weekly deep clean

Even with daily rinsing, fine fibers and mineral deposits build up in the screen.

1. Fill a sink or basin with warm water and either 1 tablespoon of baking soda or ½ cup of white vinegar.
2. Soak the screen, auger and bowl for 15 to 30 minutes.
3. Scrub the screen again with a brush, then rinse well.
4. Hold the screen up to the light. If you can see clogged holes, repeat.

## Removing stains

Carrots, beets and turmeric can stain plastic parts orange or pink.

- **Rub with lemon juice** and leave the parts in sunlight for a few hours. UV light fades many stains.
- **Use a baking soda paste.** Mix baking soda with a little water, rub it on, and rinse.
- **Accept a little tint.** Light staining is cosmetic and doesn't affect performance.

## Getting rid of smells

Lingering odors usually come from pulp trapped in the seals or gaskets. Remove any rubber seals that your manual says are removable, wash them separately, and let everything dry fully before storing.

## Maintenance habits that extend juicer life

- **Check your manual before using the dishwasher.** Many juicer parts are top-rack safe, but heat can warp screens and augers.
- **Never submerge the motor base.** Wipe it with a damp cloth.
- **Replace worn screens.** A cracked or warped screen lowers yield and can damage the auger.
- **Store it assembled and dry**, so parts don't get lost and the seals keep their shape.

## FAQ

**Can I leave my juicer until the evening?** You can, but dried pulp is much harder to remove. If you're in a rush, at least soak the screen in water.

**What's the easiest juicer to clean?** Models with fewer parts and simple strainers are easiest. Centrifugal juicers clean fast, and some slow juicers are designed specifically for quick cleaning.
