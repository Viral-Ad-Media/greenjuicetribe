A short juice cleanse can be a nice way to reset your habits, give your digestion a lighter few days and enjoy more vegetables. It isn't magic, though. Your liver and kidneys already do the detoxing. The value is in the break from processed food and the habits you carry forward afterward.

> **Safety first:** A juice-only plan is low in protein and calories. Skip it if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, kidney disease, an eating disorder or another medical condition, or take medication, unless your doctor approves it. If you feel dizzy or unwell, stop and eat a normal meal.

## The daily rhythm

Each day you'll drink six juices about two to three hours apart, plus plenty of water and herbal tea. Each recipe makes roughly 12 to 16 oz.

| Time | Juice |
|---|---|
| 8 am | Green Start |
| 10:30 am | Citrus Glow |
| 1 pm | Green Start |
| 3:30 pm | Beet Root Boost |
| 6 pm | Green Start |
| 8 pm | Almond "Mylk" (a little protein and fat to finish the day) |

## The recipes

### Green Start
2 cups spinach, 3 kale leaves, 1 cucumber, 3 celery stalks, 1 green apple, ½ lemon, ½ inch ginger.

### Citrus Glow
2 oranges, 1 grapefruit (skip it if it interacts with your medication), 1 carrot, ½ lemon, a pinch of turmeric.

### Beet Root Boost
1 small beet, 3 carrots, 1 apple, ½ lemon, ½ inch ginger.

### Almond "Mylk"
1 cup soaked almonds, 3 cups water, 1 date, a pinch of cinnamon and a pinch of salt. Blend, then strain through a nut milk bag.

## Shopping list for all three days

- Spinach: 3 large bags
- Kale: 2 bunches
- Cucumbers: 9
- Celery: 3 heads
- Green apples: 12
- Lemons: 10
- Fresh ginger: 1 large hand
- Oranges: 6; grapefruit: 3
- Carrots: 2 lb
- Beets: 3 small
- Raw almonds: 3 cups; dates: 3

## Before and after

- **Two days before:** Cut back on caffeine, alcohol, sugar and processed foods. Eat more vegetables.
- **After:** Ease back in with fruit, salads, soups and steamed vegetables for a day or two before returning to your usual meals.

## Tips

- Make juices in the morning or the night before, and store them in full, sealed glass bottles in the fridge.
- A [masticating juicer](/best-masticating-juicers-2026/) or [hydraulic press](/best-hydraulic-press-juicers-2026/) gives better yields and keeps juice fresher for batch prep.
- Want a gentler option? Swap one juice a day for a smoothie, which keeps the fiber.
