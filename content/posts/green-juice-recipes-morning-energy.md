A glass of cold green juice is a refreshing way to wake up and get a few servings of vegetables in before breakfast. These five recipes keep the fruit low and the flavor high. Each makes about one large glass.

![Green juice recipes for morning](/uploads/2026/07/5-green-juice-recipes-for-morning-thumbnail-1.jpg)

## 1. Classic Morning Green

- 1 cucumber
- 3 celery stalks
- 1 handful spinach
- 1 green apple
- ½ lemon

The everyday green juice: mild, crisp and easy to love.

## 2. Ginger Kick

- 2 handfuls kale
- 1 cucumber
- 1 pear
- 1 inch ginger
- ½ lime

Ginger adds warmth and a little heat that wakes up your taste buds.

## 3. Citrus Mint Refresher

- 1 cucumber
- 1 orange, peeled
- 1 handful spinach
- 1 small handful fresh mint
- ½ lemon

This one tastes like a spa day. It's great on hot mornings.

## 4. Pineapple Green Glow

- 1 cup pineapple
- 2 handfuls spinach
- 2 celery stalks
- ½ cucumber
- A few sprigs of parsley

Pineapple balances the parsley and makes this a good "gateway" green juice.

## 5. Super Greens

- 3 kale leaves
- 1 handful spinach
- 1 handful parsley
- 1 cucumber
- 2 celery stalks
- ½ lemon
- 1 small green apple

This is the greenest of the bunch. Work up to it once you're used to the taste.

## Tips for a smooth morning routine

- **Prep the night before.** Wash and chop your produce and store it in a container in the fridge.
- **Juice greens first, then hard produce.** Apples and cucumbers push the greens through.
- **Pair it with protein.** Juice is light. Have it alongside eggs, yogurt, tofu or a handful of nuts to stay full.
