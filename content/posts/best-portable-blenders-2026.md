Portable blenders are rechargeable, cordless blenders that let you make a fresh smoothie wherever you are. They're perfect for post-workout shakes, travel and office lunches. They aren't as powerful as plug-in blenders, so the best ones balance blade strength, battery life and an easy-to-clean design.

## 1. Cuisinart EvolutionX Cordless

![Cuisinart EvolutionX cordless blender](/uploads/2022/10/1.-Cuisinart-EvolutionX-Cordless-Blender.jpg)

A cordless blender that feels like a real kitchen appliance, with good blending power and a sturdy jar. It's best if you want cordless convenience at home as well as on the go.

**Pros:** Strong for a cordless, sturdy build. **Cons:** Heavier than pocket-size models.

## 2. BlendJet 2

![BlendJet 2](/uploads/2022/10/Blendjet-2.jpg)

The BlendJet 2 is the portable blender most people recognize. It's light, comes in lots of colors, charges by USB-C, and can self-clean with a little soapy water.

**Pros:** Light, easy to clean, USB-C. **Cons:** Small capacity, and big frozen chunks can be hard on it.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:BlendJet 2)

## 3. Ninja Blast

![Ninja portable blender](/uploads/2022/10/NINJA-NUTRI-PRO.jpg)

Ninja's portable model is one of the stronger cordless options for frozen fruit and ice, with a leak-proof lid that doubles as a drinking spout.

**Pros:** Handles frozen fruit well, leak-proof lid. **Cons:** Larger than mini blenders.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Ninja Blast portable blender)

## 4. NutriBullet GO

![NutriBullet GO](/uploads/2022/10/Nutribullet-GO-Portable-Blender.jpg)

A compact cordless option from NutriBullet that's easy to toss in a gym bag. It's best for protein shakes and soft fruit.

**Pros:** Compact, trusted brand. **Cons:** Modest power.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:NutriBullet GO portable blender)

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Ninja Foodi smoothie maker)

## Buying tips

- **Battery and charging:** USB-C charging is the most convenient. Check how many blends you get per charge.
- **Capacity:** Most hold about one serving. Fill only to the max line.
- **Safety lock:** Look for a lock that stops the blades from running when the jar isn't attached.
- **Ingredients:** Cut fruit small and add liquid first. Portable blenders are best with soft or partly thawed fruit.
