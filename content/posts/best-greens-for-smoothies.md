Adding a handful of greens is the easiest way to make a smoothie more nourishing. Some greens disappear into the fruit. Others announce themselves loudly. Here are ten leafy picks, ranked from mildest to boldest, with tips for using each one.

![Greens for smoothies](/uploads/2026/07/greens-for-smoothies-10-leafy-picks-thumbnail-1.jpg)

## 1. Baby spinach

The best starter green. It's mild, it blends completely smooth, and you won't taste it under berries or banana. Use 1 to 2 big handfuls.

## 2. Romaine lettuce

Crisp, hydrating and very mild. It adds volume and freshness without changing the flavor much.

## 3. Butter lettuce

Soft, tender leaves that blend easily. It's a good, gentle way to add greens if spinach is unavailable.

## 4. Swiss chard

Slightly earthy, with a mild beet-like sweetness. Remove thick stems if your blender isn't powerful.

## 5. Kale

The green smoothie classic. Choose lacinato (Tuscan) kale for a milder flavor, remove the tough stems, and pair it with pineapple or mango. Frozen kale blends especially smoothly.

## 6. Bok choy

Juicy, crunchy and mild. It's an underrated green that blends well with citrus.

## 7. Collard greens

Sturdy and slightly bitter. Use half a leaf with the stem removed, and blend it well.

## 8. Parsley

Bright and herbal. A small handful lifts green smoothies with lemon, cucumber and pineapple.

## 9. Mint and basil

These herbs are more flavor than bulk. A few leaves make fruit smoothies taste fresh and grown-up.

## 10. Arugula

Peppery and bold. Use a small handful in savory or citrus smoothies. It's for when you're comfortable with greens.

## Tips for green smoothies that taste great

- **Start with 1 cup of greens** and increase gradually.
- **Blend greens with liquid first,** then add fruit and ice. This gives you the smoothest texture.
- **Balance bitterness** with sweet fruit (mango, pineapple, banana), acid (lemon, lime) or creaminess (avocado, yogurt).
- **Freeze your greens.** Frozen spinach and kale last longer and make smoothies colder and thicker.
- **Rotate greens weekly** for variety.
