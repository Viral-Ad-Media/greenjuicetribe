A single-serve blender (also called a personal blender) blends directly in the cup you drink from. If your routine is one green smoothie before work, it's faster to use and much faster to clean than a big countertop blender.

## 1. Ninja Foodi Power Nutri

![Ninja Foodi Power Nutri](/uploads/2022/10/Ninja-Foodie-Power-Nutri.jpg)

Ninja's Power Nutri is one of the strongest personal blenders around. It handles frozen fruit and kale without much fuss and comes with cups you can take straight to work.

**Pros:** Strong motor, smooth results, travel cups. **Cons:** Louder than average.

## 2. Ninja Fit

![Ninja Fit](/uploads/2022/10/Ninja-Fit.jpg)

A simple pulse-to-blend design that costs very little and does a good job on soft fruit, protein shakes and leafy greens with enough liquid.

**Pros:** Cheap, compact, easy to clean. **Cons:** Struggles with lots of ice.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Ninja Fit personal blender)

## 3. NutriBullet Pro

![NutriBullet Pro](/uploads/2022/10/3.-Ninja-Nutribullet-Pro.jpg)

The NutriBullet Pro is the classic personal blender. You push down and twist to blend, and the extractor blade does a solid job on greens and seeds.

**Pros:** Proven design, widely available parts. **Cons:** The cups wear over time.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:NutriBullet Pro)

## 4. Hamilton Beach Single-Serve

![Hamilton Beach single-serve blender](/uploads/2022/10/hamilton-beach-Single-Blend.webp)

The simplest way to get into single-serve blending. It's best for protein shakes and soft fruit smoothies rather than tough greens.

**Pros:** Very affordable, one button. **Cons:** Limited power.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Hamilton Beach single serve blender)

![Beast blender](/uploads/2022/10/beast-blender.jpg)

**Pros:** Beautiful design, strong blending. **Cons:** Premium price.

## 6. Magic Bullet

![Magic Bullet](/uploads/2022/10/MAGIC-BULLET-BLENDER-1.jpg)

The tiny Magic Bullet is ideal for dorms and small apartments. It's best for dressings, sauces and light smoothies.

**Pros:** Tiny and cheap. **Cons:** Not for frozen or fibrous blends.

![Ninja Foodi Smoothie Bowl Maker](/uploads/2022/10/Ninja-Foodie-Bowl-Maker.jpg)

Its built-in tamper lets you blend very thick, spoonable smoothie bowls without adding extra liquid. It's great for acai-style bowls.

**Pros:** Handles thick blends, includes a tamper. **Cons:** Bulkier than a typical personal blender.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Ninja Foodi Smoothie Bowl Maker)

## Tips for better single-serve smoothies

1. **Liquid first, then greens, then frozen fruit.** Layering helps the blade catch everything.
2. **Don't overfill.** Stay under the max line, or the seal can leak.
3. **Rinse right away.** A quick rinse with warm water and a drop of soap, then a 10-second blend, cleans most cups.
