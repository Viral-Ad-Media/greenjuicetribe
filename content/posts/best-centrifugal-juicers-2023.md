Centrifugal juicers spin a flat cutting disc at high speed and fling the juice through a fine mesh basket. They're the fastest way to make a glass of juice. Most have wide chutes that take whole apples and carrots, and they cost less than slow juicers.

The trade-off is that they're louder, they produce more foam, and they're less efficient with leafy greens. If your juice is mostly carrots, apples, beets, celery and cucumber, a centrifugal juicer is a great fit.

## Tips for centrifugal juicing

- **Drink it fresh.** Centrifugal juice oxidizes faster, so it's best enjoyed within about 15 to 30 minutes.
- **Bundle greens.** Roll leafy greens into tight bundles, or sandwich them between apple pieces, to get more juice.
- **Skim the foam.** Many juicers come with a foam separator. You can also pour the juice through a small strainer.

Juicing mostly greens? A [masticating juicer](/best-masticating-juicers-2026/) will serve you better.
