A good countertop blender is the most versatile tool in a plant-based kitchen. It handles green smoothies, frozen fruit bowls, hot soups, sauces, nut milks and nut butters. The difference between a great blender and a mediocre one shows up quickly with kale stems, frozen bananas and nuts.

These are the nine full-size blenders we'd consider in 2026.

## 1. Vitamix Professional Series 750

![Vitamix Professional Series 750](/uploads/2022/10/1.-Vitamix-Professional-Series-750.jpg)

The Professional Series 750 combines Vitamix's powerful motor with preset programs for smoothies, frozen desserts, soups and cleaning. The low-profile container fits under most kitchen cabinets. It turns kale, seeds and frozen fruit into a perfectly smooth drink and can even heat soup through friction.

**Pros:** Extremely smooth results, useful presets, long warranty.
**Cons:** Premium price, and it's loud at full speed.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Vitamix Professional Series 750)

## 2. Cuisinart Hurricane Pro

![Cuisinart Hurricane Pro](/uploads/2022/10/2.-Cuisinart-Hurricane-Pro-Countertop-Blender.jpg)

The Hurricane Pro has a lot of power and a set of useful programs at a lower price than the premium brands. It's a strong option if you blend daily but don't want to spend Vitamix money.

**Pros:** Powerful, good presets, good value.
**Cons:** Bulky base.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Cuisinart Hurricane Pro blender)

## 3. Vitamix 7500

![Vitamix 7500](/uploads/2022/10/3.-Vitamix-7500-Countertop-blender.jpg)

The 7500 is a simpler Vitamix with variable speed and a pulse switch, no presets, and a low-profile 64-ounce container. It's excellent for families and batch smoothie prep.

**Pros:** Big capacity, simple controls, Vitamix durability.
**Cons:** No programs or timer.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Vitamix 7500)

## 4. Vitamix 5200

![Vitamix 5200](/uploads/2022/10/4.-Vitamix-5200-Countertop-Blender.jpg)

The 5200 is the classic tall-container Vitamix that has been around for years. The tall, narrow jar creates a strong vortex that's especially good for small batches and nut butters.

**Pros:** Proven reliability, great vortex for small batches.
**Cons:** The tall jar may not fit under cabinets.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Vitamix 5200)

## 5. Ninja Professional

![Ninja Professional countertop blender](/uploads/2022/10/5.-Ninja-Professional-Countertop-Blender.jpg)

Ninja's stacked-blade design crushes ice and frozen fruit quickly at a fraction of the premium price. It's a little less silky with fibrous greens, but it's a fantastic budget workhorse.

**Pros:** Great ice crushing, very affordable.
**Cons:** Stacked blades are awkward to clean, and the texture is less smooth with greens.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Ninja Professional countertop blender)

## 6. Oster Pro 1200

![Oster Pro 1200](/uploads/2022/10/6.-Oster-Pro-1200-Blender.jpg)

The Oster Pro 1200 often comes with a to-go smoothie cup, which makes it a two-in-one value buy. Its reversing blade action helps pull ingredients down for smoother results.

**Pros:** Great value, includes a smoothie cup.
**Cons:** Shorter lifespan with heavy daily use.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Oster Pro 1200 blender)

## 7. KitchenAid 5-Speed

![KitchenAid 5-speed blender](/uploads/2022/10/7.-KitchenAid-5-Speed-Countertop-Blender.jpg)

A stylish, reliable blender for occasional smoothies, sauces and shakes. It comes in many colors and works well for light to moderate use.

**Pros:** Attractive, easy to use, compact.
**Cons:** Not built for daily heavy blending.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:KitchenAid 5-Speed blender)

## 8. Blendtec Total Classic

![Blendtec Total Classic](/uploads/2022/10/8.-Blendtec-Total-Classic-Countertop-Blender.jpg)

Blendtec's square jar and blunt blade are built around one-touch programs that you start and walk away from. It's very powerful and easy to clean.

**Pros:** Powerful, convenient presets, durable.
**Cons:** There is no tamper, so very thick blends need extra liquid.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Blendtec Total Classic)

## 9. Ninja MasterPrep

![Ninja MasterPrep](/uploads/2022/10/9.-Ninja-MasterPrep-Countertop-Blender.jpg)

The MasterPrep is a compact blender and chopper for small kitchens and simple drinks. It isn't for daily green smoothies, but it's great for salsas, frozen drinks and quick prep.

**Pros:** Cheap, compact, versatile.
**Cons:** Limited power for fibrous greens.

[Check price on Amazon](amazon:Ninja MasterPrep)

## What to look for in a countertop blender

- **Power and blade design:** Fibrous greens and nuts need strong motors and well-designed jars, not just high wattage numbers.
- **Container size and height:** Measure the space under your cabinets before buying.
- **Programs vs. variable speed:** Presets are convenient. Variable speed gives you more control.
- **Warranty:** Premium blenders often come with long warranties that make the higher price easier to justify.
