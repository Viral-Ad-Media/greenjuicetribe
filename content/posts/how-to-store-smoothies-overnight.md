Blending the night before is one of the easiest ways to make healthy mornings stick. A smoothie stored correctly stays tasty for about 24 hours. The two enemies are **air**, which causes browning and flavor loss, and **time**, which causes separation.

![Store smoothies overnight](/uploads/2026/07/store-smoothies-overnight-losing-flavor-or-thumbnail-1.jpg)

## The short answer

Pour your smoothie into a glass jar, fill it **right to the top**, seal it tightly, and refrigerate it. In the morning, shake or re-blend for 10 seconds, then drink.

## Step by step

1. **Add an acid.** A squeeze of lemon or lime juice slows browning, especially with banana, apple and avocado.
2. **Use the right container.** Use an airtight glass jar or bottle, like a mason jar. Glass doesn't hold onto odors, and a full jar leaves little room for air.
3. **Fill to the brim.** The less air in the jar, the less oxidation.
4. **Refrigerate immediately** at 40°F (4°C) or below.
5. **Shake or re-blend before drinking.** Separation is natural and harmless.

## Ingredients that store well, and ones that don't

**Store well:** berries, mango, pineapple, spinach, kale, oats, nut butters, protein powder, chia seeds (which thicken overnight, in a good way).

**Store less well:** banana (it browns and the flavor changes), apple and pear (they brown), yogurt-heavy blends (they get thick and tangy), and ice (it melts and waters the smoothie down).

## The freezer method (for up to a few months)

- **Freeze blended smoothies** in jars, leaving about an inch of space for expansion. Thaw them overnight in the fridge.
- **Freeze smoothie cubes** in ice cube trays, then blend the cubes with a splash of liquid in the morning.
- **Make smoothie packs.** Portion chopped fruit and greens into freezer bags. Dump a pack into the blender with your liquid. This is our favorite method for the freshest taste.

## How long do smoothies last?

| Storage | Time |
|---|---|
| Fridge, airtight and full | Up to 24 hours (best within 12) |
| Freezer, blended | 1 to 3 months |
| Freezer, unblended packs | 3 to 6 months |

If a smoothie smells sour or fizzy, throw it out.
