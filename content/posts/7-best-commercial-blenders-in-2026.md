## What to check before buying a commercial blender

- **Duty cycle:** How many blends per hour the motor is rated for without overheating.
- **Jar material and size:** BPA-free copolyester jars are standard. Choose 48 to 64 oz for most shops.
- **Noise:** A sound enclosure matters in a café where customers are talking.
- **Certification:** For a business, check local food-equipment requirements such as NSF certification.
- **Parts and service:** Spare jars, blades and quick repairs keep you running.
