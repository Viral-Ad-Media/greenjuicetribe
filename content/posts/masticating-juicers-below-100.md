## Getting the most from a budget slow juicer

- **Cut fibrous produce short.** Celery and kale stems should be cut into 1 to 2 inch pieces to stop strands from wrapping around the auger.
- **Alternate soft and hard produce.** Feed in an apple between handfuls of greens to push them through.
- **Clean right away.** Dried pulp in the strainer is what makes cleanup painful.
