Your first green juice shouldn't taste like lawn clippings. This recipe starts with mild, hydrating vegetables and balances them with apple, lemon and a little ginger, so it's bright, refreshing and easy to like.

![Green juice for beginners](/uploads/2026/09/green-juice-recipe-for-beginners.jpg)

## Ingredients (makes about 2 cups)

- 1 large cucumber
- 3 celery stalks
- 1 to 2 green apples
- 2 big handfuls of baby spinach
- ½ lemon, peeled
- 1 small piece of fresh ginger (about ½ inch), optional

## Juicer method

1. Wash everything well. Cut the produce to fit your juicer's chute.
2. Juice the spinach first, then follow it with the harder produce (apple, cucumber) to push the greens through.
3. Juice the celery, lemon and ginger.
4. Stir and serve over ice, or drink right away.

## Blender method (no juicer needed)

1. Roughly chop everything and add it to a blender with ½ cup of cold water.
2. Blend on high until completely smooth.
3. Pour through a nut milk bag or fine sieve, and squeeze out the juice.

## Make it your own

- **Too grassy?** Add a second apple or a few slices of pineapple.
- **Too sweet?** Swap one apple for more cucumber.
- **Ready for more greens?** Add a leaf or two of kale, building up over a couple of weeks.
- **Want more zing?** Add more lemon and ginger, or a pinch of fresh mint.

## Beginner tips

- **Go mostly vegetables.** A ratio of roughly 80% vegetables to 20% fruit keeps sugar in check.
- **Drink it fresh.** Juice tastes best and keeps its color best when you drink it soon after making it. If you need to store it, fill a glass jar to the brim, seal it, and refrigerate it for up to a day.
